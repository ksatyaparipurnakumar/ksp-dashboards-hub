      Building back resilient (CEA Task Force)

      • Double-pole structure with AB switch every 10 poles – first on SKP HQ, Pallipalem, Mori, KD Palem, Gondi Industrial.
      • Spun / rail poles and intermediate poles on rebuilt spans.
      • Plinth-mounted 3-Ph DTRs – Gondi Industrial (31 DTRs); stronger 1-Ph structures – Rameswaram (8).
      • AB cable for LT near the sea – Antarvedi, Appanaramuni Lanka.
      • RCC / pre-cast foundations in paddy and aqua bunds; epoxy coating against salt corrosion.
      • GPS mapping of every DTR and pole; split long feeders (KD Palem 17 km).
      • Permanent 50-person restoration shelter at each 33/11 KV SS; 250-person village shelters.

      Feedback and reporting system

      One format, two reporting times, one owner per line
      • 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM – each officer-in-charge reports feeder, team, poles erected, spans strung, DTRs charged, services restored, labour, machinery, material short and help needed.
      • Control room consolidates feeder-wise and SS-wise status in the cyclone data format the same hour; follows up a missing report within 30 minutes.
      • 8:00 PM – Mandal review with the MRO; Overall In-charge sends the mandal report to Division and Circle (BRK Circle Arnab format: SS / feeders pending, villages, damages, dumping, batches, labour, officer, crane and PDM).
      • Village level – village secretary and volunteers report unrestored houses by WhatsApp; complaint register logs each with time closed.
      • Evidence – GPS-stamped before/after photos for every pole location and DTR, kept feeder-wise.
      • WhatsApp group per SS and one mandal group carry the reports.
