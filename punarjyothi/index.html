    Material chain

    Damage enumeration→Requirement (area / DTR-wise)→Indent→Receipt at sub-store→Issue to batch→Utilisation at pole / DTR→Balance → next indent

    Every step is one line in a register (templates 03–08, 20). Unused material is devolved back and accounted item-wise after the restoration.

    Source: AP common Standard Schedule of Rates, 2021-22. Prefer spun poles on rebuilt spans of the worst-hit feeders.

    Procurement rules

    • Split pole orders type-wise across several approved manufacturers; no single factory sets the pace.
    • Ask each manufacturer for a daily dispatch quantity and the nearest delivery point.
    • Record ordered, dispatched, received and balance daily; late orders turn red.
    • Plan lorries and cranes from weight, not count.
    • Keep Annual Rate Contracts in coastal areas so material and workforce come without tender delay.
    • Record spares on the DRIPS portal; review the material bank before every cyclone season.
    • After restoration, devolve unused material and arrive at quantities material-wise.

    Daily rhythm

    Reporting system

    • 10 AM and 4 PM – officer-in-charge reports: poles, spans, DTRs, services, labour, machinery, material short, help needed (template 14).
    • Same hour – control room consolidates feeder-wise and SS-wise; chases a missing report within 30 minutes.
    • 8 PM – review chaired with the revenue authority; next day's plan and machinery moves.
    • Village level – unrestored houses reported by volunteers; complaint register logs time closed.
    • Evidence – GPS-stamped before/after photo for every pole and DTR.
    • Micro – span-by-span on the feeder sketch. Macro – % of km per feeder across the area.
